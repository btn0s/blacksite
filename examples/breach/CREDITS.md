## CC BY 4.0

Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The first-person arms are the Blacksite found field set from Sketchfab. The rifle is [AKM Assault Rifle - Animated](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/akm-assault-rifle-animated-6348dc53e3e249c6b297cb8f3f1e3e9b) by [user77](https://sketchfab.com/user77), which combines arms and animations from [fps AK animated](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fps-ak-animated-b0b1bae40337449189483f6187cf7af7) by [DJMaesen](https://sketchfab.com/bumstrum) and the [AKM](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/akm-fe8b79ae8a8142fb9ba706912c6b9df8) model by [Armored Wave](https://sketchfab.com/armoredwave). The pistol is [animated pistol](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-pistol-bd896167e7ca44f19597d3afe6a8d83f) by [DJMaesen](https://sketchfab.com/bumstrum). Blacksite re-baked the files, reduced textures to 1024-pixel JPEGs, removed emissive maps and retoned the sleeves olive; keyframes are unchanged. Breach plays their reload, empty reload, holster and draw clip lengths as its Armory timings. Full attribution, changes and the licence text ship with the game at `assets/viewmodel/found/CREDITS.md` and `assets/viewmodel/found/CC-BY-4.0.txt`. These credits do not imply endorsement.

## Breach surface and processing notes

Old Planks 02 by Rob Tuytel (Poly Haven): https://polyhaven.com/a/old_planks_02 — [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Resized to 1K colour/normal maps and packed occlusion, roughness and metalness.

The recordings above are selected takes, trimmed, normalized and encoded to Opus with AAC alternatives. Breach uses four rifle takes, three pistol takes and four takes for each recorded footstep surface. Room tails, impacts, handling, radio cues and ambience are synthesized in the project.

The shared Blacksite world materials, fonts, character and the earlier arms rigs retain their upstream credits in the [Blacksite site credits](https://blacksite-sable.vercel.app/CREDITS.md) and packaged asset notices.
