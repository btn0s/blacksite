## Traverse processing changes

Recordings were installed with `blacksite assets add`, normalized and encoded as Opus with AAC fallback. Traverse retains five original takes for each of its four footstep surfaces. Playback decodes at 24 kHz. Wind and water-drip recordings become three excerpts each with 400 ms wrap crossfades; the original encoded recordings are retained in `assets/audio`. Surface landings layer the recorded step with a synthesized low thump. Source hashes and attribution are retained in `assets.credits.json`.
