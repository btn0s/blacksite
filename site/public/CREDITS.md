## First-person viewmodel

The default arms derive from the **MakeHuman team**'s bundled [MPFB mesh, weights and skeleton](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2), [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md). Blacksite crops, dresses, rigs and textures the arms; its original sleeve geometry is also CC0. The application's source-code licence does not cover exported mesh output, as explained in [MPFB's licence statement](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/blob/master/LICENSE.md).

The default rifle derives from **LonesomeDucky**'s [AKM](https://opengameart.org/content/akm), **CC0**, including its weathered PBR maps. Blacksite adds a part rig, changes orientation and reduces the embedded maps to 1024-pixel JPEGs. The default pistol derives from **Quaternius**'s [Animated Guns Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/animatedguns.html), [CC0](https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-animated-guns), with adapted scale, orientation and part rig. The sleeve, glove and pistol surface textures listed above are by **ambientCG / Lennart Demes**, [CC0](https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/).

`viewmodel/found-pistol.glb` is based on **[9mm Pistol | First Person Animations](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/9mm-pistol-first-person-animations-c26d7f5aa72f4b01a6da4578caa8f07f)** by **[1Matzh](https://sketchfab.com/1Matzh)**, **CC BY 4.0**. It incorporates **[9mm Pistol](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/9mm-pistol-30222f9a59104426ba526a6b20cd7532)** by **[Urpo](https://sketchfab.com/Urpo)** and arms edited from **[Modern Soldier](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/modern-soldier-358b4fb07f0146cb9b9063342db5897a)** by **[Blue Spirit](https://sketchfab.com/Blue-Spirit)**, both CC BY 4.0. Blacksite re-exported the file and reduced textures to 512-pixel JPEGs. [Full component attribution and changes](viewmodel/CREDITS.md); [full licence and warranty disclaimer](viewmodel/CC-BY-4.0.txt). This credit does not imply endorsement.
