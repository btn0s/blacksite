The default first-person arms in the Firing Range are CC BY 4.0 works from Sketchfab, restyled by Blacksite. [Full licence and warranty disclaimer](CC-BY-4.0.txt). No endorsement is implied.

- **Rifle**: [AKM Assault Rifle - Animated](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/akm-assault-rifle-animated-6348dc53e3e249c6b297cb8f3f1e3e9b) by [user77](https://sketchfab.com/user77). It combines arms and animations from [fps AK animated](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fps-ak-animated-b0b1bae40337449189483f6187cf7af7) by [DJMaesen](https://sketchfab.com/bumstrum) and the weapon model [AKM](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/akm-fe8b79ae8a8142fb9ba706912c6b9df8) by [Armored Wave](https://sketchfab.com/armoredwave). All three are CC BY 4.0.
- **Pistol**: [animated pistol](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-pistol-bd896167e7ca44f19597d3afe6a8d83f) by [DJMaesen](https://sketchfab.com/bumstrum), CC BY 4.0.

Changes: Blacksite re-baked both downloads, trimmed them to the deforming joints and animated parts, reduced textures to 1024-pixel JPEGs, removed emissive maps and retoned the sleeves olive. Keyframes are unchanged. Sway, bob, recoil, aiming, sprint and the rifle inspect are added procedurally.
