# Found pistol comparison

`found-pistol.glb` is based on **9mm Pistol | First Person Animations** by **1Matzh**, [source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/9mm-pistol-first-person-animations-c26d7f5aa72f4b01a6da4578caa8f07f), [author](https://sketchfab.com/1Matzh), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](CC-BY-4.0.txt).

The component works are **9mm Pistol** by **Urpo** ([source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/9mm-pistol-30222f9a59104426ba526a6b20cd7532), [author](https://sketchfab.com/Urpo)), rigged by 1Matzh, and **Modern Soldier** by **Blue Spirit** ([source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/modern-soldier-358b4fb07f0146cb9b9063342db5897a), [author](https://sketchfab.com/Blue-Spirit)), edited to arms by 1Matzh. Both are CC BY 4.0.

The source presentation additionally credits **sky dome demo** by **fangzhangmnm** ([source](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/sky-dome-demo-a5778d3c90e644e2be1062895ccd44aa), CC BY 4.0), using Poly Haven's CC0 [Je Gray Park](https://polyhaven.com/a/je_gray_park). The shipped comparison contains only the arms and weapon meshes.

Blacksite re-exported GLB, resized the textures to 512 pixels, encoded them as JPEG quality 85, and removed an unrelated preview object. The six original clips are `Idle`, `Walk`, `Run`, `Fire`, `Reload` and `Reload_Empty`.

The comparison player preserves the authored poses and curves but plays `Reload_Empty` at approximately 1.2533× speed, reducing its duration from 3.9167 to 3.125 seconds to match the gameplay reload timer. The normal `Reload` remains 3.125 seconds.
