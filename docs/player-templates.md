# Player templates

| Profile | Use when | Game mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fpp` | Single-player first person | Arms-only `@blacksite/subject-fpp` and Armory on the template walker, with Rapier ray hits |
| `tpp` | Single-player third person | ALS body and camera driven from `@blacksite/input` |
| `mp` | Multiplayer first and third person | `MultiplayerScene` with local arms, remote ALS bodies, Colyseus and the offline loopback |
| `blank` | A custom player | The template walker in the starter yard |

Every profile scaffolds the same shared parts in `src/game/`: `model.ts` (feel and budget numbers, read by the game and the check), `input.ts` (one `@blacksite/input` context for keyboard, mouse, gamepad and touch), `stage.tsx` (the canvas, `@blacksite/look` with `autoQuality()`, fixed-step physics and the starter yard), `check.tsx` (the `window.__blacksite` contract), `sound.ts` (`@blacksite/soundscape`) and `hud.tsx` (hints, the click-to-play prompt and touch controls). The game boots straight into play; Pause holds Settings, Main menu and the prototypes. A fresh scaffold passes `pnpm check` and `blacksite check dist` on desktop. See [Set up](../skills/blacksite/references/setup.md).

The CLI scaffolds `mp` with a local Colyseus room under `pnpm dev`; Play joins through the same-origin `/relay` path and falls back to the loopback when the room is unreachable. A production build without `VITE_RELAY_URL` plays on the loopback; set it to the deployed room endpoint. Open a second tab to join the online room. The local camera starts in FPP with rigged ALS arms (rifle and pistol; see [arms](arms.md)), and V switches to TPP; set `cameraMode="fpp"` or `cameraMode="tpp"` on the subject to lock a game's camera. The template passes `input="external"` and drives the subject with `driveSubject` and `pressSubject`. Remote players always use ALS bodies, and camera choice stays local. Vite accepts `*.getbb.app` and `*.ts.net` preview hosts by default; `VITE_ALLOWED_HOSTS` adds comma-separated hostnames. [Game architecture](../skills/blacksite/references/architecture.md) explains the ownership model and transport choices. For a static two-player demo, mount `LoopbackSplitView` from `@blacksite/subject`; it creates two connections through `createLoopbackRoom()` from `@blacksite/relay` and renders both canvases. Relay v0 has full snapshots, owner movement, and Armory-validated hits; see [Relay limits](../packages/relay/AGENTS.md).
