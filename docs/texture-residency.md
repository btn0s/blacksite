# Courtyard texture residency

The courtyard uses version 2 scan surface descriptors. Each built image has immutable KTX2 tails at maximum 256, 512, 1024 and 2048 dimensions, with dimensions, mip count and wire bytes. `buildScanMaterialTails` in `examples/shared/build-scan-materials.mjs` builds these descriptors from an existing published surface set. It copies encoded mip levels and their metadata; it never decodes or re-encodes images. A new owned build is published by changing only `courtyard.json`'s `surfacesUrl`, after the loader has been merged.

`ScanTextureStreaming` owns texture identities, shared texture nodes, requests, allocations and retirement. Every image starts with its minimum tail. Promotion and demotion allocate a replacement, upload it, swap the existing nodes, and keep the old allocation charged until a completed selection snapshot proves its last frame has finished. Lower demand must remain cold for three seconds. Promotion admission preserves enough overlap for the largest minimum-tail replacement. Stale requests are aborted; failed requests retain the current tail.

The shared GPU ledger has a 2560 MiB ceiling, a 768 MiB texture ceiling and a 64 MiB queued upload ceiling. It charges the current geometry buffers and texture-feedback buffers, resident and retiring textures, pending texture allocations and upload reservations. Decode staging has a separate 64 MiB CPU ledger. Texture bytes use the negotiated format's block dimensions and all mip levels. Renderer targets, programs and unchanged character assets remain outside this ledger and are reported by the existing inspection snapshot.

One replacement is admitted per frame, with at most 8 MiB uploaded. This adapter's maximum is 2K; formats whose 2K tail exceeds the upload unit receive a smaller maximum. Scheduling and retirement stop at a 0.5 ms bookkeeping quota. Upload submission time is recorded separately as `Texture tail upload` performance measures and in inspection telemetry.

`ClusterTextureFeedback` derives UV0 and UV1 density bounds once on the GPU from the existing quantized triangles. The bound is the reciprocal of the larger of the minor UV gradient and the major gradient divided by the sampler's 8x anisotropy. This also handles rank-deficient UV mappings without an infinite inverse. Demand uses selected cluster bounds and placement scale, authored UV tiling, the smart layer's clamped placement scale and world-space noise mapping. No placement scan runs on the CPU.

The selection integration is the optional `MeshletSceneOptions.textureFeedback` contract. `MeshletScene.readStats` reads one combined snapshot of the selection counters and atomically reduced mip demand. It uses three reusable public `ReadbackBuffer` slots, skips sampling within four frames or when all slots are busy, and never awaits feedback on the render tick. Snapshot completion supplies the retirement serial. Slice 2 can call this same snapshot reader from its bounded feedback cycle; it must not add an independent texture polling cycle. Traversal and compaction are unchanged by this slice.
