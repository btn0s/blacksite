# Open asset source research

Checked 16 October 2026 against publisher pages, licence texts and record metadata. This is the evidence behind the catalog, not a blanket approval of every file on each host. The [tracked-file audit](audit.md) is separate. A candidate below is not installed unless it has a catalog entry and a successful local build. Visual comparisons and measured audio notes belong in the asset viewer and the accompanying report; no ear audition is claimed here.

## Rights and acquisition

The library needs commercial use, modification, public display and redistribution of the asset files themselves. [CC0's legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en) provides a permanent waiver and fallback licence. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) permits sharing and adaptations with attribution, a licence link and an indication of changes. Keep supplied notices. An open licence on a photograph does not grant trademarks or a person's publicity rights.

Downloads and copyright are separate. An asset may be CC0 while its host requires an account or restricts API access. Blacksite's manual entries use a user-supplied original; they do not scrape authenticated sessions. Hashes pin the reviewed download, not the truth of its uploader's copyright claim.

## Gunfire and weapon foley: the download gap is resolved

[The Free Firearm Sound Library](https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library) is a preserved copy of recordings by Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney. It links CC0 and reproduces the authors' statement, “Our team holds CC0 NO RIGHTS RESERVED for this library.” The source page explains that the original site is archived, and identifies OGA as a mirror, not the producer. The catalog pins OGA's prepared archive by SHA-256. Describe this as a licensed source/mirror download rather than claiming every URL is the original creator's host.

The selected recordings cover AK-47 near singles, bursts and middle distance, Walther PPQ 9 mm, 1911 .45 and Nova shotgun. Their separate distances support a believable mix without applying the same close report everywhere. The original files contain multiple events and quiet handling sounds; onset detection alone can mistake handling for shots. Review the selected take times and preserve the report's acoustic tail. A numeric waveform check is not an ear audition.

[Distant rifle recordings](https://zenodo.org/records/14824927) are by Jakob Wirén and Kasper Nordenram. The [record API](https://zenodo.org/api/records/14824927) explicitly specifies `cc-by-4.0`; `AR150m.zip` is a small, separately downloadable archive. These 16 kHz field recordings suit remote combat, not a close player weapon.

The authors' OGA pages mark [handgun reload](https://opengameart.org/content/handgun-reload-sound-effect) and [shotgun operation](https://opengameart.org/content/shotgun-reload-sound-effects) by zer0_sol as CC0. [Equipment Clicks III](https://opengameart.org/content/equipment-clicks-iii) by LFA is CC0 and describes a mixture of actual bolt-action handling, stapler and tape-measure sounds. Select the appropriate event instead of calling every click a rifle recording.

## itch.io checks

Six manual entries remain separate from starter packs. All six live publisher pages were rechecked. The stopped thread's history records archive inspection and embedded licence text; those temporary archives are no longer retained, so no claim of a fresh archive re-audit is made. Before promoting one into a pack, lock the actual supplied archive and retain its licence evidence. The catalog's logical archive names need not match the creator's current download label when using `fetch --from <single-file>`.

| Publisher page | Page grant / inherited embedded evidence | Selection note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [PSX Soviet Block Kit Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-soviet-block-kit-free) | CC BY 4.0 / `LICENSE.txt` says the same | 15 free pieces; code-generated, AI-assisted. The separately sold full pack has its own licence and is not included. |
| [PSX Soviet Apartment Props](https://flipperside.itch.io/psx-soviet-apartment-props) | CC0 / `LICENSE.txt` names CC0-1.0 and redistribution | AI-assisted graphics; 48,241-triangle bed needs reduction. |
| [Footsteps SFX Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/footsteps-sfx-free) | CC BY 4.0 / `LICENSE.txt` names CC BY 4.0 | Synthesised in code, not recorded foley. Thirty samples cover more surfaces than the previous four-surface summary suggested. |
| [PSX Horror SFX Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-sfx-free) | CC BY 4.0 / `LICENSE.txt` names CC BY 4.0 | Deliberately band-limited synthetic retro sound; not the realistic gunfire default. |
| [Horror SFX Volume 1](https://obsydianx.itch.io/horror-sfx-volume-1) | CC BY 4.0 link / README licence link | Drones/stingers; page discloses no generative AI. Select a take before treating a mixed pack as one ambience. |
| [Nox Essentials](https://nox-sound-design.itch.io/essentials-series-sfx-nox-sound) | CC0 / `Essentials_Series_README.pdf` names CC0 | Recorded footsteps/voices/ambiences; 1,644 WAVs. Useful manual expansion, too large to fetch casually. |

Manual acquisition is a current library policy, not a claim that itch universally requires sign-in. Generic “free for commercial use” wording is insufficient when standalone redistribution is unstated.

No external accounts, uploads, purchases or mirror publication were needed for this research.
