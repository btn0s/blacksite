# Prop credits

Every prop is a photoscanned model from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/models), released under [CC0 1.0](https://polyhaven.com/license). `scripts/fetch-props.mjs` downloads the 1k glTF, welds and simplifies the mesh, rests it on the origin, encodes its textures as KTX2 (ETC1S colour and ORM, UASTC normals) and compresses it with meshopt. Rigs and animations are dropped, so every prop is a static pose. Sizes are in metres and match the dimensions Poly Haven lists; `shelving` was authored at ten times that and is scaled down.
