# Look

Purpose: post-processing and atmosphere for three.js scenes. `createLook(renderer, scene, camera, options)` from `@blacksite/look` owns an EffectComposer (render, N8AO, bloom, grade) plus fog, a procedural sky dome, an optional PMREM sky environment, and optional sun and fill lights; call `render(dt)` instead of `renderer.render`. `@blacksite/look/r3f` exports `<Look />`, which takes over R3F rendering with a priority 1 `useFrame`. Atmospheres are plain data (`ZONE`, `HAZE`, or your own `Atmosphere`); quality tiers are `high`, `medium`, `low` with `autoQuality()`. The renderer runs with `NoToneMapping` because the grade's shoulder curve is the tonemapper. LUTs are generated in-repo by `scripts/make-lut.mjs`; never copy LUT images from elsewhere. three is a peer; React and R3F are optional peers for `/r3f`. See `README.md`.
