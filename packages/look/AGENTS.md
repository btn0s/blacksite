# Look

Purpose: post-processing and atmosphere for three.js scenes. `createLook(renderer, scene, camera, options)` from `@blacksite/look` owns an EffectComposer (render, N8AO, bloom, grade) plus fog, a procedural sky dome, an optional PMREM sky environment, and optional sun and fill lights; call `render(dt)` instead of `renderer.render`. `@blacksite/look/r3f` exports `<Look />`, which takes over R3F rendering with a priority 1 `useFrame`. Atmospheres are plain data (`ZONE`, `HAZE`, or your own `Atmosphere`); quality tiers are `high`, `medium`, `low` with `autoQuality()`. The grade's shoulder curve is the only tonemapper and `atmosphere.exposure` the only exposure; the renderer runs with `NoToneMapping`, and `createAtmosphere` in the world kit never sets tone mapping or exposure. LUTs are generated in-repo by `scripts/make-lut.mjs`; never copy LUT images from elsewhere. three is a peer; React and R3F are optional peers for `/r3f`. `@blacksite/look/world` is the ruins world kit: every `SurfaceName` is a 512 px KTX2 bake of a `@blacksite/materials` scan graph in `assets/surfaces/` (rebake with `scripts/bake-surfaces.mjs`), streamed in place so `surface()` stays synchronous; one surface shader blends grime, damp and soot from the bake's masks and world position (height above the room floor, nearness to the ceiling and to openings set with `weatherSpaces()`), and also carries the ground occlusion; `LevelBuilder`, ground and puddle materials, and props (apartment blocks, Ladas, sheds, water towers, the APC, dead trees). See `README.md`.
